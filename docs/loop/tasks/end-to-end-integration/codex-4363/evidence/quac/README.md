# Additional model-free source check

QuAC contains human information-seeking dialogues whose recorded answers are passage excerpts or the corpus's unanswerable marker. The authors distribute TRAIN and validation under CC BY-SA 4.0 and retain a hidden test set. We inspected only public TRAIN and preserved the official validation split for independent confirmation. The research screen uses exact native tokenization, causal role messages and original span offsets. [QuAC authors and data](https://quac.ai/)

The [unsplit coarse screen](unsplit.json) gave 1,187 disjoint pairs before full serving and contamination checks. The [registered filters](FILTER.md) subsequently left 3,484 clean rows and 138 witnessed pairs globally. The original component-plus-wording split gave 107 training pairs and zero held pairs. Holding out only by component supplied 16 held pairs ahead of family isolation. The largest single loss came from the 1,088-ID full-conversation cap. [Filtered supply](filtered.json) · [Independent graph audit](filtered-graph.json)

The first implementation of the historical prompt screen passed a synthetic empty initial user field into a check that expects real text and rejected all 45,902 positive candidates. The corrected call passes canonical first-user content. Both attempts are retained on Wick; the aggregate here comes from the corrected run. Source selection on this public corpus supplies no production donor authorization or model-quality result.

In an exploratory follow-up we first packed 138 intact, component-disjoint pairs and allocated complete normalized target-question families at roughly 4:1. We then removed any pair whose history contains a target-question family from the opposite split. This left 101 TRAIN and 26 internally held pairs across 18 and 21 families, respectively. One training family supplies 83 pairs. The largest held family supplies three. Of the 26 held pairs, 11 have the same original turn ordinal and zero compare the registered near and far strata. The final roster contains no shared component, target family or opposite-side target-family mention in retained question histories. Full question histories, source offsets, canonical messages, original IDs and natural EOS are in the private manifests.
