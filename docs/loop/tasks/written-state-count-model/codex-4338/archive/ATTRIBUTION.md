# Dataset attribution

The archived natural questions and passages come from SQuAD 2.0, published by
Pranav Rajpurkar, Robin Jia, and Percy Liang. The adapted natural tapes use
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International, the dataset's
license. Each source paragraph comes from Wikimedia contributors to the named
English Wikipedia article. Complete original dataset files and source records
retain the article title and published annotation structures.
[SQuAD 2.0](https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/) ·
[Original TRAIN](https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/dataset/train-v2.0.json) ·
[Original DEV](https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/dataset/dev-v2.0.json) ·
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

The new roster pairs two original DEV questions in one original paragraph or
contiguous original excerpt. It joins each passage and question with a space
and adds exact source coordinates, protocol-2 tokens, and frozen partitions.
TRAIN supervision adds cited, separately adjudicated labels. The prior study's
exposed inputs retain their original attribution and source identifiers.
