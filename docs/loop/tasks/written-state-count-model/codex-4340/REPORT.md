# The held article coverage failed before the matched weight fit

## What did not work

[codex] Three of the eleven answerability titles yielded fewer than the predeclared minimum of two defensible, reachable source/NoRead contrasts. The 1973 oil crisis supplied zero, Harvard University one and Islamism one. Each had four fixed candidates. The two source assessors made those ceilings apparent before the ordinary client ran. The coverage decision is instrument-inconclusive. The four weight arms were never fitted, and the two locked panels stayed sealed. [Registered stop](SHEET.md) · [Title counts](evidence/validation-coverage.json)

[codex] On the oil-crisis passage about macroeconomic problems, one reader accepted the annotated adjective as a reply and the other required the complete noun phrase. Another excerpt named the consequences of scarcity and admitted two readings of a question about price controls. For another pair, both readers agreed that the named groups could not match Kissinger's dominance. They disagreed whether changing the subject to the Middle East made the question unanswered or gave it a specifically false premise. The registration required agreement on that distinction. The oil-crisis result is a property of these four reviewed pairs and the exact rule. [Source adjudication](validation/REGISTRATION.md) · [Consensus rule](validation/consensus.jq)

## What changed

[codex] Starting from [the merged TRAIN qualification](../codex-4338/REPORT.md), we staged the unchanged integer CUDA trainer, all four matched training tapes, label-permuted controls and label-blind device score tapes on Wick. The public preregistration preceded validation selection. Seven two-answer article strata had 1, 2, 7, 9, 10, 18 and 30 available passages. The fixed sample takes up to four per title, giving 23 two-answer and 44 answerability validation pairs. Both assessors read the same exact client-visible text. They had no model readout and did not consult each other. Each returned 67 passage- and case-bound judgments with individual reasons. No decision used a model outcome.
