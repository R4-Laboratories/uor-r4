# Isolate which update destroys the selector switch

Balanced remains the integration baseline. Preserve both fitted residuals, their 6,144 signed slot traces, and the raw outputs. The joint model learned useful stop actions on individual prompts and completed 16/64 training contexts. On fresh wording, every one of its 128 selector-only pairs took the same first action on both members. Even with the first choice supplied, it took the same post-B action on both members of every two/three-word direct pair. These paired failures give a more discriminating target than another total-reply count.

**First, use the weights and signed updates already paid for.** The [signed write audit](evidence/write-accounting.json) already reconstructed all 4,096 final residual words from the two sets of logged contributions. Both stages wrote 402 of the same addresses, including 205 with opposing net changes. Use those verified components to produce separate period-write-only and B-write-only residuals alongside their measured sum and the zero control. Use the ordinary frozen geometric scorer and real writer to measure when a period STOP/COPY score crosses zero for both members of each known failing pair. Test the symmetrical question at B on two/three-word pairs after an authenticated B. Treat the exposed held wording as diagnostic. A comparison made by adding logged contributions is conditional on this realized training trajectory: replaying training with those other slots absent can change which margins trigger. This check needs no new optimizer sweep.

**Let that trace decide the next weight change.** If post-B writes erase correct period flips, compare shared and stage-disjoint residual addresses on untouched wording. Match total trainable words, update opportunities and stage-by-stage target counts, with identical pair-balanced first-stage training in both arms. If period updates alone already fail to distinguish opposed requests, inspect the geometric addresses that differ *within the same selector pair*. Test a signed paired margin on those distinguishing addresses, on both wordings under both clause orders; score the same construction on previously unseen authors and families. For a same-score collision with identical visited addresses, revisit the instruction representation before another weight-only fit. Include direct two/three controls where correct authenticated B is common and the desired release changes. A universal stop policy gets exactly half of that mixed panel's individual decisions and zero paired wins.

Require more fully correct held contexts, correct signed flips on both axes, direct two/three paired wins after verified B, and preservation of old passing IDs. Add a small separately reported ordinary free-response panel to monitor whether progress reaches actual dialogue when moving beyond the supplied-source and supplied-opener setting. Source discovery and opening generation remain independent product gates.
