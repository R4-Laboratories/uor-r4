## Draw and execution

Split the 3,602 full launches into four rank intervals with floor boundaries at (N j/4). ## Measurements and decision

Capture process-cold artifact validation/load, CUDA setup and arm initialization separately. Record rank bin, materialized and unclipped depth distributions, full/sliding rows, target counts, resets, scheduler, coordinate schedule, materialization, device baseline evaluation, exact rows-K score/reduction/readback, CPU choose/apply, conditional joint evaluation and total wall by arm and launch. Report snapshot work, diagnostic overhead and observed host and GPU resources separately. Clocks are synchronized host-wall measurements of the original stage calls, including transfers, launch and readback. Filesystem page cache can be warm on an already provisioned host. Compare the raw scheduler census against its host-only receipt. A complete and consistent sample supplies measured stage cost and a bounded throughput estimate for full launches. A parity, count, timing or custody failure yields an instrument finding. Scheduling and DEV-tune overhead of epoch convergence have no measured total here, and the 1,722 partial launch timings remain unmeasured.
