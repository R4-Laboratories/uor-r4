# Ordinary whole-turn admission from matched supervision

Lab codex. Development experiment for #4063 after #4328 and the donor inventory in #4329. The product goal is a native integer language model that gives useful self-fed answers on ordinary devices. This unit tests the first original-address decision. No supplied opener, answer form or gold position is available to the client.

**Prediction.** A learned margin on the complete current user turn can recover more positive-source/NoRead question pairs on unseen articles than the frozen framed reader, while reducing false copies relative to exposing the same whole turn with frozen words. A geometric test here can fail even if the required source and supervision are available. Check whether TRAIN pairs change when interpreting a failure.

Compare the old framed reader, whole-turn frozen words and whole-turn fitted words. New artifacts wrap the same parent and 513 signed i16 words. Original current-message boundaries come from the ordinary serializer. Source admission occurs only at the first actual transition. No forcing of a space or use of an answer prefix.

**Fit.** Use 114 human TRAIN pairs whose positive is eligible immediately and 240 authored TRAIN rows, exactly 468 examples. Order by stable case identity. Four full epochs from the extracted frozen state, including an independent repeat from those same bytes. On a positive, oppose the strongest competing source or NoRead. On an unsupported row, oppose the highest-scoring candidate. Below margin 8, move the active signed integer coordinates by one unit, cancel overlapping feature increments and saturate to ±4096. In training, the earliest original address breaks tied lexical rival scores, with NoRead preferred as the positive rival on equal score. In serving, a copy requires one unique best source strictly above NoRead. A tie between lexical sources abstains. Expose every arm to the same evaluation tape. Only the trained arm updates weights. The comparison of frozen and fitted states isolates those updates. Record exposures, active writes and total changed words. EVAL, article identities, gold addresses and reference output must be inaccessible to the optimizer except for the designated TRAIN target. No held tuning.
