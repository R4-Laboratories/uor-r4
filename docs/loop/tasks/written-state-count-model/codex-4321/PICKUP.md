Keep the #4321 artifact, writer, exact copy and both prior results as controls. Start on TRAIN: 132 of 192 first-period pairs selected the same action on both sides, even though none had equal scores. The pair-difference term fired just once across all eight epochs at that stage. A wide difference can coexist with two positive scores. The single-row stage-zero training stream also contains three copy requests for each stop request.

Compare the registered training schedule against two isolated changes on the same TRAIN subset: balance the opposing first-period actions within updates, and directly update whichever member of the pair misses its own signed margin. Cross the two factors. Review class score distributions, both signed margins, paired actions and full self-fed replies on TRAIN and a fresh development wording split before opening another held panel. Require exact-ID and cold-state parity and preserve the working two-versus-three word-count controls.

Reserve phrasing families and facts, and distinguish new wording types from repeated fact contexts.
