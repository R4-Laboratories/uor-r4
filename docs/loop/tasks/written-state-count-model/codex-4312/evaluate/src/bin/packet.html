Blind conversation ratings

Read the conversation and judge each reply to the final user message independently. Answer as a person who might have asked that question. Use the same standard for both replies, even when they are identical.

Rating guide and source

0 · Unusable. The reply is empty, unintelligible, materially wrong, or fails to address the request. 1 · Partial. It offers relevant help but leaves a material gap, error or unfinished thought. 2 · Useful. It answers the request correctly enough to use, respects the conversation and is complete. Give appropriate clarification questions credit when a direct answer would require missing information.

Consider correctness, relevance and clarity. A polished opening alone earns no credit. Model answers appear in full. “EOS” means the model chose to stop; “token limit” means generation ended at the fixed cap. Use “Cannot judge” where specialist knowledge is required. Rate each answer before choosing a preference. Fill out the packet independently. Download your ratings to save or return them. The page sends nothing over the network.

Conversation histories come from the English OpenAssistant Conversations Dataset (OASST2) by LAION/OpenAssistant contributors, Apache License 2.0. The two replies were generated for this study. Dataset and attribution.
