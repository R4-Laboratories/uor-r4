# What moved the two target scores

This readback uses the frozen natural geometry actual table. It rebuilt 42,776 signed scores over 264 TRAIN prompts and 19,838 over 116 development prompts from the exact 4,096 signed words and captured candidate keys. Every score and selected address matched both the ordinary client and CUDA. Both target margins matched the sealed paired-margin readback on every side. The private detailed rows and all input bindings are in the Wick `banks-v1` seal. The public report contains only aggregates. [Aggregate bank counts](../evidence/banks.json)

## The two forms of aliasing

For the left question, write its signed feature contrast as (x(q_L,A)-x(q_L,B)). For the right question the desired order is reversed, so its contrast is (x(q_R,B)-x(q_R,A)). The sum of those two vectors determines whether any additive table can shift relative preference in the desired direction.

Within a question, the two target actions never had identical full feature vectors in the 264 TRAIN or 116 development openings. Across questions, the signed contrasts canceled exactly on 1 of 72 reciprocal and 4 of 60 answerability TRAIN pairs, and on 2 of 15 reciprocal and 1 of 43 answerability development pairs. Every additive weight table on the same coordinates fails at least one strict target comparison on those pairs. A further answerability development pair tied in the fitted relative score with distinct signed contrasts. These two types of zero have different implications.

Each of the 16 zero route effects in the development table came from exact cancellation of the route-key contrast. The full score canceled identically on three of those pairs. The changing routes themselves took the wrong net sign on seven reciprocal and eleven answerability pairs. On reciprocal questions, bank 10 configured from the query anchor, source anchor and token left of the candidate supplied 49 positive and 10 negative effects on TRAIN, then 2 positive and 8 negative on development. Its address contrast changed on 11 of the 15 development pairs. This is a signed ordering failure on distinct addresses.

On the two reciprocal development pairs that strictly beat both intended mates, a third interval blocked three sides and NoRead blocked the fourth. ## Bounded next experiment

Use a fresh, blinded relation-preserving question paraphrase panel to test the current query-anchor × source-anchor × left-context route against one anchor-transport variant with an explicit signed query-to-source displacement. Freeze candidate boundaries and original addresses before creating question variants. Check that the route remains stable when the wording changes but the requested relation stays the same, and changes when the requested relation reverses. Predeclare the exact within-question aliases, across-question cancellation and original target-versus-strongest-rival checks before fitting. Evaluate the existing frozen table's signed margins as a baseline. This distinguishes inability to encode the reversal from a learned sign that changes with lexical context. The present development counts nominate a probe and supply no evaluation data for its acceptance.
