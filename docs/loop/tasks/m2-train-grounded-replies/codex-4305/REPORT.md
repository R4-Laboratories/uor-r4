## Still open

The native selector still has to infer the supplied relations from text. These results identify two narrower tests. First, on governor-after wording, hold governor and candidate addresses fixed and change which argument is actually acted on by asserting or clearing the fallback condition. Compare native scope against correct and swapped conditional states, keeping the alternatives legal on both prompts. Second, on matched wordings that recover the contested noun, independently supply the opposite signed governor and the opposite argument sites to find what recovers the full tuple. Keep the heading free, retain both occurrences and all real rivals, and keep checking all 28 historical pairs. The current wins cover the specific new wording/fact panel and concentrate in two families. [Held family breakdown](evidence/outcomes.jsonl)

## Plain summary

When the model got the governor and a list of real candidate objects, it followed the first/later instruction on 15 pairs. The exact occurrence was still the model’s decision. On governor-after wordings the conditional alternative won every time. When the requested occurrence did win, the second signed attachment still prevented a complete read for the writer.
