# Independent paired source audit

The preflight reads only original input and archived physical captures. It checks the 64
fit rows, the eight distance-matched rows and the 48 protected near rows against sealed
predecessor identities. The near mask is rederived from all 48 independently decoded
original prompts in the #4299 premise. Each protected pair must contain its original
`defer` and `take` prompt, byte and absolute BPE coordinates for both signed governors,
and all three source occurrences. The graded and compact device masks must agree.

The verifier reuses the independent #4300 address decoder, integer packet loader and
native joint replay. Its own reduction groups all frozen triples by the site occupied
by the inspected sign. A fixed-governor group chooses the exact authored governor at
that site and independently minimizes each opposite attachment. A free group also
minimizes over all strictly signed nonoverlapping governors at the inspected site. For
each legal triple the joint cost is the sum of the two lexical costs, the sum of the
two transport costs, and the frozen bank sum, compared in that order. Opposite and
same-sign minimizing assignments multiply within a free triple and sum across tied
triples. The verifier keeps every tied triple ordinal and signed governor address.

The strongest third excludes both named same-spelling sites. It is recorded with
both the authored and unrestricted governor policies, even when it loses the overall
competition. A same-cost third, an unresolved native tuple and a site with no completion
each have a distinct representation. A signed-source win requires a unique normal
tuple, the original signed governor and the requested site in both original byte and
BPE coordinates. A full win requires both signed governors and all three source sites
on both selector variants.

`run-wick.sh` builds the independent verifier from a pinned inherited source closure
and applies `train-census` to the sealed TRAIN inputs. `archived-near` compares both
starting states to the prior physical captures. `score-finite` tests the physical
scorer and `fit-finite` verifies both synthetic integer states against the inherited
physical oracle. The finite state tool verifies inactive sentinels and creates a view
for the older parser. It uses a new file and leaves the native evidence bytes intact.

`train-election.sh` checks every captured state against its TRAIN, regression and
near traces, independently derives the exact pair identities and election metrics,
and reproduces the complete election order. `fresh-replay.sh` checks the physical
readout of the four frozen states and the supplied and swapped original-address
controls. Its two intersection files cover the selected initialization and the
updated diagnostic respectively, both against the two archived baselines. Each file
gives original paired wins and losses overall and by family for signed and full metrics.

All model replay commands require previously captured GPU output. The synthetic Rust
tests exercise an alternate governor exposing a masked third win, all tied third sites,
a same-site governor tie, an absent third and a row with no eligible completion.

The archived near replay agrees on 4,848 scalar fields per state. The original
ordered state retains 16/24 named pairs and misses exactly eight `supplier-contract`
pairs. Its ordered-median successor retains all 24. The fixed #4300 matched TRAIN
record independently verified the original exact signed switch on 4/4 and the
median on 0/4.

The physical TRAIN replay agrees on 6,104,277 scalar native and competition fields
across 79 states and reproduces the complete election order. Both admissible states
are initial blends. The elected state `median-bilateral-3` keeps all 28 protected
pairs. The best updated state `fit-5-6` keeps all 24 near pairs and misses all four
selector regressions. It earns its own diagnostic lane.

The fresh replay agrees on 990,770 scalar fields across the four frozen lanes. Each
lane has 0/256 correct signed source rows, 0/128 correct signed pairs and 0/128 full
pairs. Both interventions nominate a legal complete tuple on all rows. Supplied exact
original links recover 256/256 signed and full rows, and the same-spelling swaps
recover zero. In every model lane the intended governor is ineligible for 32 requested
links. On the other 224 rows the strongest unrestricted third tuple outranks the
requested tuple at the correct governor. The replay recovers all minimizing tuples
and governor ordinals for the site-group witnesses rather than only the first tuple.
