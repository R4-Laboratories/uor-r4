# Where the distant governor disappeared

## What did not work

Neither representation selected both exact original source triples on any of the 128 reserve pairs under strict ranking. On the 128 distant-governor endpoints, the two opposite assignments had identical input points. The main record previously left open whether the two distinct governing token sequences had reached the projection.

## What changed

The source reconstruction follows the two earlier BPE occurrences and each governor on the original tape. For the starting and alternate projections it records the selected token IDs, their original locations and their phases, then compares the reconstructed native words to the submitted packets. [Measured coverage and source](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/pull/4283)

## Measurements

The 64 endpoints with a 14-token gap and the 64 with a 20-token gap all omitted the governor tokens from both noun occurrences' four sampled contexts. The governor sequences differed on every endpoint. At the nearby governors, both occurrences sampled their own governor in the right-hand and combined contexts on all 128 endpoints. Both source readers still lost those 128 nearby rankings. [Main experiment report](../codex-4284/REPORT.md)

## Checks run at the head

The replay matched 98,304 native i16 coordinate words. The counts cover two occurrences, two representations and three 32-word projections on each of 256 endpoints. The ordinary measurements belong to the primary D11 comparison. The alternate representation ended at its registered source-ranking bar.

## Skills used

The native-space cycle guided the exact integer readback. The research-architect skill guided the distinction between selecting evidence and ranking addressed sources.

## Still open

## Plain summary

The two instructions used different words, but neither word reached its noun on the distant examples. Changing prototype weights cannot distinguish two identical inputs.
